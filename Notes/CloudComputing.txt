Deployment models of cloud:-
Private cloud:-  Singel org not exposed to public,complete control,security
Public Cloud:- Owned by third party
Hybrid Cloud:- some onpremises some on cloud platform

Types of Cloud Computing:-
1.IaaS:- Infrastructure as a Service (provide networking,computers and data storage space in its raw form)
2.PaaS:- Platform as a Service ( just focus on the deployment and management of your applications.)
3.SaaS:- Software as a Service (completed product that is going to be run and managed by the service provider)
