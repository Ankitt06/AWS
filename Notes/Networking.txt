Client ---- Network ---- Server

Explanation of client-server architecture.
Clients have IP address and servers also have ip address using which they communicate  over the network

Key components of servers: CPU, RAM, and long-term storage solutions (databases).

Network: bunch of cables, routers, and servers that are going to be connected with each other
Router: is a specific device that will forward the data packets between computer in the networks, and they will know where to send your packets on the internet
switch: will send the packet to the correct clients on your network.

All togather 
Client ----- Router ---- Switch -----send data to the requiered computer
